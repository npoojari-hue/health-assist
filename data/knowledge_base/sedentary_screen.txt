TITLE: Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines - Sedentary behaviour
ORG: CSEP / PHAC
URL: https://csepguidelines.ca/guidelines/adults-18-64/

Sedentary time means time spent sitting or lying down while awake, such as at a desk, in a vehicle or watching a screen. For adults, a limit of 8 hours or less per day is encouraged.
Recreational screen time (for example watching shows or gaming, not work) should be 3 hours or less per day.
Breaking up long periods of sitting with short breaks to stand, stretch or walk is encouraged. Even light movement during these breaks is helpful.
