TITLE: Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines for Adults (18-64 and 65+)
ORG: CSEP / PHAC
URL: https://csepguidelines.ca/guidelines/adults-18-64/

Sleep: Adults aged 18 to 64 are encouraged to get 7 to 9 hours of good quality sleep on a regular basis, with consistent bed and wake-up times. Adults aged 65 and older are encouraged to get 7 to 8 hours of good quality sleep.
Physical activity: Adults should get at least 150 minutes of moderate to vigorous physical activity each week. Adding muscle-strengthening activities using major muscle groups on at least 2 days per week gives extra benefit. Several hours of light physical activity, including standing, is also encouraged.
Sedentary behaviour: Adults are encouraged to limit sedentary time to 8 hours or less per day. Within that, recreational screen time should be limited to 3 hours or less, and long periods of sitting should be broken up when possible.
Together: Replacing sedentary time with more physical activity, and trading light activity for more moderate activity while keeping enough sleep, can give health benefits. The guidelines are for the general adult population and are not personal medical advice.
