TITLE: WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour (adults)
ORG: World Health Organization
URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity

For adults aged 18 to 64, WHO recommends at least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or at least 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity, or an equivalent mix.
Adults should also do muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity that involve all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week.
Adults should limit the amount of time spent being sedentary. Replacing sedentary time with physical activity of any intensity, including light activity, gives health benefits.
Some physical activity is better than none. People who cannot meet the recommendations can start with small amounts and slowly build up over time.
