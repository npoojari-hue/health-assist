TITLE: Canada's Food Guide - Healthy eating recommendations
ORG: Health Canada
URL: https://food-guide.canada.ca/en/healthy-eating-recommendations/

Canada's Food Guide encourages people to make water their drink of choice. Water quenches thirst, has no calories and costs little. There is no single exact daily amount for everyone, because needs depend on the person, the weather and activity.
The Food Guide suggests eating plenty of vegetables and fruits, whole grain foods and protein foods, and choosing plant-based protein foods more often.
It also suggests limiting highly processed foods and drinks that are high in sodium, sugars or saturated fat, and being mindful of eating habits and cooking more often at home.
